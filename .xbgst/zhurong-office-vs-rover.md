# Dispatch result — Zhurong: 火正 office vs Mars-rover namesake
**Protocol:** v0.2 · **Effort:** low · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Scope:** entire project · **Posture:** additive

## Goal
Which Zhurong belongs in Plazir teachings: hearth/south-office 火正 or CNSA rover namesake? One primary citation, one claim, one rejected alt.

## Artifact
**Primary belongs: Zhurong-as-火正 (office), not the rover.**

**Primary citation (one):** *Shiji* 40 · 楚世家:
「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融。」
(Chongli held the office of 火正 under Emperor Ku/Gaoxin, with great merit, able to brighten the world; Emperor Ku named him Zhurong.) obs · certain

## Claim (one)
Zhurong in the teaching codex is the **bestowed fire office, not a deity and not a spacecraft**: a title conferred for merit on an office-holder (and inherited by his brother Wu Hui), so fire is *stewardship held*, never property owned (obs · certain — Shiji 40; plan-zhurong.md scout-00). The south/hearth association (Liji 月令: 其帝炎帝，其神祝融; Huainanzi 時則: 南方…其佐祝融) is downstream of the same office tradition and stays in scope as framing (obs · strong). This is the only reading that adds the missing axis — **tenure of held power** — to Siduri (limit), 1337b (end), Sekhmet (force) (inf · strong).

## Rejected alternative
**CNSA rover 祝融 (2021) as primary coordinate.** Rejected: the rover *borrows* the name; it is a naming event, not a source text. Its official gloss — 寓意點燃我國星際探測的火種, landed Utopia Planitia 2021-05-14/15 — is a coda echoing the Fire Star (火星), and citing it as primary would ground the teaching in a 2021 PR act instead of the civilizational text it derives from (obs · certain — matches plan-zhurong.md scout-02 rejection: "rover-first rejected as primary"). Keep as textual-note coda only.

# Claim — Zhurong-as-火正 is the tenure axis
**Artifact:** 01-claim · **Session:** 0 · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Status:** advisory (pre-judge)
**Axes:** groundedness · non-duplication · coverage (3 nodes) · minimality

## Goal
How Zhurong-as-火正 (Shiji 40) connects Siduri finitude, 1337b schole, Sekhmet dual-force — without duplicating any. One claim + one rejected alt.

## Claim (one)
Zhurong-as-火正 binds the three teachings as **stewardship of held power**: fire as *office*, not property — named by another, transmissible, surviving the officer. obs · confidence: strong
Evidence: Shiji 40 楚世家 「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融。」 obs

- **Siduri (finitude)** — she sets the bound: death dispensed to mankind, joy inside it. obs
  Zhurong is tenure *inside* the bound: not deathlessness (Gilgamesh's error) but a held charge that outlives the holder. inf · strong
- **1337b (schole)** — leisure, not amusement, is the end; play is medicine. obs
  火正 is occupation that is not banausic because it is not for itself: 能光融天下 — held fire keeps a world where schole is possible. inf · strong
- **Sekhmet (dual force)** — "do not smash — aim it." obs
  Sekhmet is aim at the moment of *release*; Zhurong is aim at the moment of *appointment* — 帝嚳命曰祝融, force under title. inf · strong

No duplication: the other three answer **limit**, **end**, **force**; Zhurong alone answers **tenure** — how held power is carried. Remove it and the codex has no doctrine of carrying. inf · strong

## Rejected alternative
**Prometheus fire-theft/gift as the connective frame.**
- Rejected because gift-theft is an *event*, not an office: no successor, no tenure, nothing that survives the officer. It lands in Sekhmet's smash/aim slot and contributes zero new axis → pure duplication. obs (plan-zhurong.md risk: "Prometheus / Shanhaijing-as-executioner collapse Zhurong into Sekhmet's smash slot") · certain
- Subsidiary rejects (one line each): Zhuangzi 養生主 — wrong speaker, wrong book, not Zhurong. obs · CNSA rover 祝融 as primary — 2021 Fire-Star coda, not the civilizational text; keep as coda only. obs

# Claim — Zhurong is the hearth left behind, not the shore walked past
**Artifact:** shore-claim · **Session:** 0 · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Status:** advisory (pre-judge)
**Axes:** groundedness · non-duplication · coverage (4th node) · minimality

## Goal
Gilgamesh wanders past the human world; is the Zhurong insertion a journey to an uninhabitable shore that still has a hearth? One claim + one rejected alt.

## Claim (one)
No — the insertion is the inverse of Gilgamesh's journey: Zhurong is the **hearth that stays** in the inhabited world, not a crossing to the shore past it. obs/inf · confidence: strong

- Gilgamesh walks *past* the bound of the human world seeking fire that never dies (immortality); Siduri's counsel at the edge of the sea is the codex's statement that the bound holds — the life he seeks cannot be found there. obs (siduri/ page, README)
- Zhurong-as-火正 never leaves. Shiji 40 楚世家: 「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融」— the fire is a magistracy conferred at court, held, then transmitted: after Chongli is put to death, his brother 吳回 Wu Hui inherits the post **and the name** (「使其弟吳回代復，居火正，是為祝融」). obs · certain
- The uninhabitable shore is exactly where a hearth cannot be *held* — only escaped (Utnapishtim's fire is survival, not succession). Zhurong's doctrine is that the hearth outlives the holder **by tenure inside the bound**, not by arrival past it. inf · strong
- So the insertion completes the codex on the near side of the sea: Siduri = limit, 1337b = end, Sekhmet = aim, Zhurong = **carrying**. Remove it and there is no doctrine of what survives the wanderer. inf · strong

## Rejected alternative
**"Yes — Zhurong is a journey to an uninhabitable shore"**: the fire-bringer reading (Prometheus gift/theft; 山海經 fire-god; rover-as-voyager) that makes Zhurong a crossing figure carrying fire past the edge.
- Rejected because gift-theft is an *event* with no successor: nothing is held, nothing transmitted, so it duplicates Gilgamesh's quest slot and Sekhmet's force/aim slot and contributes zero new axis. obs (plan-zhurong.md risk: Prometheus/Shanhaijing collapse Zhurong into the smash slot) · certain
- Subsidiary: CNSA rover 祝融 on Utopia Planitia (2021-05-14) is the one "shore with a hearth" reading that is literally true — but it is a 2021 coda on the Fire Star, not the civilizational text; keep as textual-note coda only. obs

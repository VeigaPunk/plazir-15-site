# Axis resolution — south / summer / hearth / office
**Artifact:** axis-answer · **Protocol:** v0.2 · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Effort:** low

## Question
Sekhmet already holds scorch/mend (dual force). Does Zhurong duplicate it, or add an axis — and which of south/summer/hearth/office?

## Claim (one)
**No duplication: Zhurong adds the OFFICE axis.** Of the four candidates only 火正-office is primary-textual and doctrinally new: Shiji 40 楚世家 「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融」 — fire as magistracy: bestowed by another (帝嚳命曰), transmissible (弟吳回襲後), survives the officer. obs · certain
Sekhmet aims force at the moment of *release* (scorch→mend); Zhurong aims force at the moment of *appointment*. Same verb, different slot: tenure/stewardship of held power, which limit (Siduri), end (1337b), and force (Sekhmet) leave unanswered. inf · strong

## Rejected alternatives
- **South + summer** — Han correlative accretion (Liji 月令 / Huainanzi 時則: southern direction, summer season, fire phase mapped onto Zhurong). Coordinates fire in *cosmology*, not in *doctrine*: adds a calendar slot, derives from the office name, and cannot answer "how is held power carried." Later systematization, not the civilizational text. inf · moderate
- **Hearth** — folk-cult reduction (domestic fire; weak/contested textual anchor). Collapses Zhurong into fire-as-object; no appointment, no successor, no tenure → zero new axis. inf · moderate
- **(Prior) Prometheus gift-theft** — event, not office; lands in Sekhmet's smash/aim slot → pure duplication. obs · certain (see zhurong-claim.md)

# Scout 4 — Zhurong on the 1337b axis: schole/paidia, fire-as-craft vs fire-as-leisure
**Artifact:** scout-answer · **Protocol:** v0.2 · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Effort:** low · **Posture:** additive

## Goal
Map Zhurong onto the Politics 1337b axes (schole/paidia). Fire-as-craft vs fire-as-leisure. One claim + one rejected alt.

## Anchors (obs)
- 1337b page (repo `1337b/index.html`): b30–34 "leisure is preferable and the end"; b35–42 "play belongs amid occupations… the person who labors needs rest, and play exists for relaxation… as one administers a medicine"; Jowett line "the first principle of all action is leisure." obs
- Shiji 40 楚世家 (frozen coordinate, `.xbgst/dispatch-zhurong-title.md`): 「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融」 — office (居火正), merit, bestowed name, transmissible to 吳回. obs

## Claim (one)
**Zhurong sits at neither pole of the schole/paidia axis: as 火正 he is the middle term — ascholia, fire-as-craft held rightly *for the sake of* schole.** inf · strong
- Fire-as-craft is the tenure; fire-as-leisure is its telos. 居火正 is occupation (ascholia), but it is not banausic in Aristotle's sense because it is not practiced for itself: 能光融天下 — held fire keeps the world in which leisure becomes possible. This is 1337b's "work rightly" (ὀρθῶς ἀσχολῆσαι) as a doctrine of carrying: craft under title, never for the craftsman. inf · strong
- Paidia is excluded from Zhurong rather than embodied by him: play is medicine *within* labor — the relaxation from exactly the 火正's work, administered at the fitting time, never the end. inf · strong
- Slot bookkeeping: Siduri = limit, 1337b = end (schole), Sekhmet = force/aim, Zhurong = the craft-side tenure that makes the end reachable. Removing Zhurong leaves schole unproduced — the codex would have an end with no doctrine of the work that sustains it. inf · strong

## Rejected alternative
**Zhurong-as-paidia: fire-as-festival/spectacle (fireworks, festal flame, fire-god-as-amusement).** inf · certain
- Shiji 40 carries zero play grammar: office, merit, bestowal (帝嚳命曰), succession (吳回襲後) are all the grammar of holding, not of amusement. obs · certain
- Aristotle subordinates paidia to rest/relaxation (anapausis), below both schole and the occupation it relieves; mapping Zhurong onto play demotes him beneath the very office he holds. obs · strong (from 1337b b35–42)
- Play is held by no one and transmitted to no one → deletes the tenure axis → collapses into the event slot (Prometheus gift-theft), already rejected in zhurong-claim.md as Sekhmet duplication. obs · certain

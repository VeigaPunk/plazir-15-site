# Claim — Shiji 40 火正 is the tenure axis
**Artifact:** claim · **Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Posture:** additive, independent derivation

## Claim (one)
Zhurong-as-火正 connects the three teachings as **stewardship of held power**: fire as *office*, not property — named by another, transmissible, surviving the officer. obs · strong
Grounding: Shiji 40 楚世家 「重黎為帝嚳高辛居火正，甚有功，能光融天下，帝嚳命曰祝融。」 (obs, from .xbgst/plan-zhurong.md scout-00); repo teachings: siduri/ = joy within finitude, 1337b/ = schole not amusement as telos, index.html L294 Sekhmet echo = "do not smash the power — aim it." obs

- **Siduri (finitude):** she sets the bound — death dispensed to mankind, joy inside it. Zhurong is tenure *inside* the bound: a held charge that outlives the holder, not deathlessness (Gilgamesh's error). inf · strong
- **1337b (schole):** leisure, not amusement, is the end. 火正 is occupation that is not banausic because it is not for itself — 能光融天下: held fire keeps the world in which schole is possible. inf · strong
- **Sekhmet (dual force):** aim at the moment of *release* (scorch/mend). Zhurong is aim at the moment of *appointment* — 帝嚳命曰祝融, force under title before it is ever wielded. inf · strong

**Non-duplication:** the three answer **limit**, **end**, **force**; Shiji 40 alone answers **tenure** — how held power is carried. Remove it and the codex has no doctrine of carrying. inf · strong

## Rejected alternative
**Prometheus fire-theft/gift as the connective frame.** Gift-theft is an *event*, not an office: no successor, no tenure, nothing that survives the officer; it lands in Sekhmet's smash/aim slot and adds zero new axis → pure duplication. inf · certain (corroborated: plan-zhurong.md risk note)
Subsidiary rejects: Zhuangzi 養生主 — wrong speaker, wrong book, category error (obs · certain); CNSA rover 祝融 as primary — 2021 Fire-Star coda, not the civilizational text; keep as coda only (inf · strong).
